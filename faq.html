FREQUENTLY ASKED
QUESTIONS

>   Where can I purchase your products?

You can shop with us through our Shopee page, Law of Attraction. Send us a direct message for product inquiries, orders, and assistance. Follow us on Instagram, @inbloomfashionph, to see our latest collections and new arrivals.

>   What sizes are available?

Sizes may vary depending on the style. Check the measurement chart provided in the product description to get the best fit. If you're unsure about the sizing, feel free to contact us before placing your order.

>   What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payments via GCash, Maya & bank transfer. Payment details will be provided upon confirming your order.

>   How long does it take to deliver my order?

Delivery times vary depending on your location and courier. Once your order has been delivered we will provide you with the tracking information.

>   Can I return/exchange an item?

We can accept returns/exchanges if you have received the wrong/damaged/defective item within 7days of receiving your order. The item must be unopened & in original condition. Please have your order details ready & clear photos of your item.

>   Are your items on-hand or pre-order?

Most items on our page are on-hand and ready to ship. For pre-order items, please allow 7 days for preparation before your order is shipped out.
